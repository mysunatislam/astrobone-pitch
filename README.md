# AstroBone: 240-Second Presentation

**Open the presentation:** https://mysunatislam.github.io/astrobone-pitch/pitch.html

Team AstroBone (Bangladesh) · NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 · Challenge: *Create Health Monitoring Software for Astronauts on Space Missions*.

The page plays like a 3:56 video with the live AstroBone app inside it: on-device pose tracking, the musculoskeletal and cardiovascular digital twin, the daily self-check and the Impact Lab. Record it with a screen recorder and add the voiceover.

## Recording the Video

1. Open the link in Chrome on a laptop or desktop, ideally a 1920×1080 screen. Keep **only one** presentation tab open; each tab loads its own pose model and 3D models.
2. Wait about 10 seconds on the start screen while it loads.
3. Press **N** to open the teleprompter in a second window (move it to another screen). Press **F** for fullscreen, **H** to hide the controls, then **Space** to start.
4. Other keys: **← / →** jump 5 s, **1–4** jump to WHO / WHY / WHAT / HOW, **Space** pauses.
5. It runs 3:56. Cut the start screen so the uploaded video stays **under 4:00**; the guide cuts longer videos off.
6. English subtitles are required: import [`docs/pitch-subtitles.srt`](docs/pitch-subtitles.srt) in the video editor and nudge any caption that runs ahead of or behind the voiceover.
7. Voiceover script and timings: [`docs/pitch-voiceover.md`](docs/pitch-voiceover.md) (the teleprompter shows the same script).
8. Upload to YouTube and put the link in the Bangladesh Google Form before the deadline.

**Offline:** after one visit the page also works without internet (the Offline scene shows the cached files). To show it on camera, open the page once online, switch on airplane mode and reload.

## What This Repository Is

A static build of the presentation from the AstroBone project, published so the team can open and record it from any computer. The application source is kept in the team's project repository.
